Today, I kept on trying to find an easy DIY way of heat sealing TPU. I took apart a heat sealing device(Fig. 1), and I observed the following components:

1. Heat sealing strip (Fig. 2) : it had a thin strip of metal that I would later find out is chromium-alloy wire, which was the core element that momentarily heats up to melt and seal the plastic. Above this wire was a Teflon tape.
2. Teflon tape (Fig. 3) : It had a heat-resistant, non-stick strip that sat over the heat sealing metallic strip to prevent the plastic bag (in this case, our TPU) from sticking to the wire.

![Fig. 1](images/pptp/heat-seal-tpu/fig1-IMG_2684.jpeg)
![Fig. 2](images/pptp/heat-seal-tpu/fig2-IMG_4304.jpeg)

Taking the heat sealing device apart, I reasoned I could find an easier DIY way of heat-sealing different plastic fabrics with customized designs easier and efficiently . For that, here are the suggested items:

1. Soldering Iron: This would be the device to apply heat.
2. Heat sealing strip (Fig. 4): For longer straight edges, this item would be placed on the TPU, then the soldering iron would be placed on the heat sealing metal allow. Because it has teflon, the TPU wouldn’t get stick to it.
3. Teflon tape (Fig. 5) : Another way to the above approach would be to tap the tip of the soldering iron with teflon tape and apply it to the TPU in any fashion one would want. The teflon would not allow the TPU to get stuck on the tip of the soldering iron.

![Fig. 4](images/pptp/heat-seal-tpu/fig4-IMG_2682.jpeg)
![Fig. 5](images/pptp/heat-seal-tpu/fig5-IMG_2681.jpeg)
