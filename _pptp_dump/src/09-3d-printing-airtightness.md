3D printing the pneumatic manifold was time consuming particular for the following reasons:

- For a system as this, ensuring airtightness was of extreme importance.
- The slightest leak of air at the manifold affected how the valves responded by closing or opening for balloons to be inflated.

While I waited for the aluminium (Fig. 1) pneumatic manifold to arrive, I designed and printed different manifold blocks. Check this [Github Repo](https://github.com/r-0n/Postgraduate-Practical-Training-Course-PPTP-) for all the parts I designed and 3d printed.

![Fig. 1](images/pptp/3d-printing-airtightness/fig1-image.png)
![Fig. 2](images/pptp/3d-printing-airtightness/fig2-image.png)

When the aluminium manifold arrived, I now had to order a pneumatic stopper and air connect fittings with the right dimentions to fit the manifold holes. Here were the exact things I purchased:

- 1/4" NPT Plug Pipe Plug Fitting Carbon Steel Male Thread Internal Allen Head Pipe Plug Set (Fig. 3)
  - Amazon Link: [https://www.amazon.ae/dp/B08NCTD7Y1?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1](https://www.amazon.ae/dp/B08NCTD7Y1?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1)
- 12 Packs 1/4" NPT Push to Connect Air Fittings Kit - 6mm to 1/4 Inch Male Thread Quick Connect Pneumatic Couplers, Heavy-Duty Universal Air Compressor Adapters (Fig. 4)
  - Amazon Link: [https://www.amazon.ae/dp/B0F5NV22GY?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title](https://www.amazon.ae/dp/B0F5NV22GY?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title)

![Fig. 3](images/pptp/3d-printing-airtightness/fig3-image.png)
![Fig. 4](images/pptp/3d-printing-airtightness/fig4-image.png)

With these pneumatic tools, I was able to achieve 85% airtightness. It was hard to achieve 100% airtightness because I realized air escaped from the plastic fittings (the blue part) of the push-to-connect fittings.

Thus, I worked around that. The Xiaomi Portable Electric Air Compressor I had been using wasn’t enough for the entire system regardless. There was a need for me to move to a more powerful air compressor.
