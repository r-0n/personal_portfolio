# Intro

The Lexivon soldering iron uses a butane tank to supply fire to the soldering bit. The one we ordered came without it so I had to wait a while to figure out where to get a butane refiller. After some research and asking around, I figured out it could be found at any vape store or Buqala because they sell lighters.

NB: Butane gas is the same gas used in lighters.

# Setting UP

### Setting up the Lexivon Butane-powered Soldering Iron.

Before you do anything, read the manual of the tool. It uses a butane tank to supply heat to the soldering bit; thus, it must be handled with care because butane is highly flameable. The manual gives instructions on how to refill, light it up, control heat/flame, etc.
