With series of testing, I realized the pressure it generated wasn’t enough or suitable for object with high volume. Thus, I transitioned to a High Volume Low Pressure (HVLP) air compressor. The one available at my disposal was borrowed from Dustin Foster (Manager of the IM Lab), and it looked like the image in Fig. 1

![Fig. 1](images/pptp/hvlp-compressor/fig1-image.png)

This air compressor worked great for this project. After so many tests shown in the videos below, I was able to come to an optimized pressure that worked great with the solenoid valves:

@video[](images/pptp/hvlp-compressor/test-video_22-36-48.mp4)
@video[](images/pptp/hvlp-compressor/test-video_22-38-41.mp4)

In order to achieve smooth inflation, the air compressor must be set to 40-45 psi.

*Note:* Anything above this would blow the tubes away violently.  Anything below this wouldn’t be able to get the valves to function properly. (see notes under [Progress on Solenoid Valves](#solenoid-progress)) or the link below:

@youtube[Solenoid valve explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6GV0BAgjLb4)
